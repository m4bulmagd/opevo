## Design Context

### Users
Professional individuals and small businesses that need reliable call coverage without staffing a full-time receptionist. They use the app to keep inbound calls answered, review what happened, and manage agent behavior without operational overhead.

### Brand Personality
Calm, capable, polished. The product should feel dependable first, modern second, and avoid startup-hype language or overly flashy visuals.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-first marketing pages with clean spacing, soft blue atmosphere, restrained motion, and clear hierarchy. The intended reference is a polished SaaS landing page with a centered hero, card-based sections, and a dark footer. Avoid noisy gradients, aggressive contrast, or generic AI-neon aesthetics.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with trust before novelty.
2. Keep layouts airy, quiet, and easy to scan.
3. Use product language that is plainspoken and operational, not promotional fluff.
4. Let blue accents suggest reliability, not futurism.
5. Preserve the existing app stack and route structure instead of inventing a separate marketing framework.
